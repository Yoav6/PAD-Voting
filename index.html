PAD Voting

Probabilistic Approval with Deference

Instructions to voters

Every voter approves any options they like. Next to an approval they may also mark MP, meaning “I'll defer to whoever is leading.” Ballots are then drawn from the box one at a time, at random, and each draw goes through four steps:

  1. Original votes (OVs). Every option the ballot approved gets +1 OV, MP or not. OVs only decide who leads.
  2. Leader. The option with the most OVs so far. If the top is tied, there's no leader.
  3. Effective votes (EVs). Each option approved without MP gets +1 EV. If anything was marked MP, the leader gets +1 EV instead, once at most. No option gets more than one EV per ballot. If there's no leader, MP marks are ignored and every approved option gets an EV.
  4. Stop. The first option to reach 2 EVs wins.

Two cases the rules leave open, settled here: if several options reach 2 EVs on the same ballot, the draw goes on until one of them holds the most EVs alone. If the box empties before anyone reaches 2 EVs, the most EVs wins, then the most OVs, then a draw by lot.
